1. Summary

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account details (name and email) and the groups, friends and expenses you add.
  • We do not show ads, sell your data, or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or payment details.
  • OmniSplit records who paid and who owes what. It does not move money or process payments.
  • Your data is stored with Google Firebase and is visible only to you and the people you share groups with.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

InformationDetails
Account information Your name and email address. If you sign up with email and password, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account.
Groups Group names, default currency, the group's members, and who created the group.
Expenses and settlements Description, amount, currency, date, who paid, how the expense was split (equally, unequally, by percentage or by shares) and each person's share. Also payments you record to settle up.
Friends The names and, optionally, email addresses of friends you add, including “guest” friends who don't have an OmniSplit account.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When the app connects to Firebase, Google's servers receive technical information that is needed to provide the service and keep it secure, such as your IP address, device and operating-system information, and app instance identifiers. OmniSplit does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not use this information to track you.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, precise or approximate location, photos or files, call logs, SMS, or bank, UPI or card details.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, and to send the email-verification and password-reset emails you request.
  • To store your groups, friends and expenses and sync them across your devices.
  • To calculate balances and suggest settlements between group members.
  • To let you find and add friends by email, and to link a guest you added to their OmniSplit account once they sign up with the email you entered.
  • To create CSV exports when you ask for them. Exported files are saved where you choose on your device or shared through the apps you pick, and we do not receive a copy.
  • To troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see your information

  • Group members: everyone in a group can see the group's name, its members' names and emails, and all of its expenses, balances and settlements.
  • Friends: when someone adds you as a friend, your name and email appear in their friends list, and theirs appear in yours.
  • Finding accounts by email: another signed-in OmniSplit user who enters your exact email address can find your account and see your name, so they can add you as a friend or group member.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

5. Service providers

OmniSplit relies on the following Google services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, email verification and password reset.
  • Cloud Firestore (Firebase) is the database that stores your profile, friends, groups and expenses.
  • Google Sign-In / Credential Manager lets you sign in with your Google account.

Their handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase. Data may be processed on Google servers in India or other countries where Google operates.

6. Information about other people

When you add friends, guests or group members, you provide their names and, optionally, their email addresses. Please add only people you actually share expenses with, and only use email addresses they would be comfortable sharing. If you were added to OmniSplit by someone else and want your details removed, ask them to remove you, or contact us.

7. Storage and security

  • Data is sent between the app and Firebase over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Database security rules limit access: you can read and change only your own profile and friends list, and only the groups you are a member of. Only a group's creator can delete the group.
  • The app keeps a local copy of your data on your device so it works offline. Changes made offline sync when you reconnect.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • You can delete expenses and friends at any time, and you can delete groups you created. Deleting a group deletes all of its expenses for every member.
  • Deleting your account: to delete your account and its data, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address registered with OmniSplit, with the subject “Delete my OmniSplit account”. We will delete your account, profile and friends list within 30 days. We will also delete the groups you created, along with their expenses, and remove you from other groups.
  • Expenses in other people's groups that involve you may stay in those groups under your name so their balances remain correct, unless you ask us to anonymise them.
  • Encrypted backups kept by our service providers may hold data for a limited time before it is permanently deleted.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it (you can change most data directly in the app);
  • have it erased;
  • withdraw consent, which you can do by deleting your account;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or are unable to do so; and
  • raise a grievance with us and, if it isn't resolved, complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

OmniSplit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Users under 18 should use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or by email. Continuing to use OmniSplit after a change means you accept the updated policy.
